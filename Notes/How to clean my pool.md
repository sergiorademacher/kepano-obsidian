## Overview

Weekly pool maintenance routine to keep water clean and safe.

## Steps

1. **Test water chemistry** - Use test strips or kit
   - pH should be 7.2-7.6
   - Chlorine should be 1-3 ppm
   - Alkalinity should be 80-120 ppm

2. **Skim the surface** - Remove leaves and debris with skimmer net

3. **Brush walls and floor** - Prevent algae buildup

4. **Vacuum the pool** - Remove settled debris

5. **Clean skimmer baskets** - Empty and rinse

6. **Check pump and filter** - Ensure proper operation

7. **Add chemicals as needed** - Based on test results
   - Add chlorine if low
   - Add pH increaser/decreaser as needed

8. **Run pump** - At least 8 hours daily

## Notes

- Best done in the morning before heavy use
- Keep chemicals in cool, dry storage
- Replace test strips every season
